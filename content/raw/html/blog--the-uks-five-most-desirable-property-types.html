The UK’s Five Most Desirable Property Types

If you could live in any property type, what would your preference be? Do you dream of a classic Victorian terrace, or perhaps an Edwardian country house? London building contractors have the answers for you.

For a quarter of Brits, Georgian detached houses are the ones we desire most. The grandeur of the great Georgian cites means that this style is always popular, and for this reason, many places with large stocks of such buildings, like Edinburgh, Bath and Brighton, tend to attract London-style prices.

The elegant style home, with symmetry and grandeur at the heart of its design, won favour over more modern builds. The top spot was closely followed by Victorian and Tudor style homes. For most Britons though, this remains an aspirational dream, owning a Regency townhouse or a Robert Adam country pile is something only to be contemplated in their wildest fantasies.

It’s harder to find similar properties in places like Glasgow and Manchester, whose architecture reflects the fact that they boomed during the reign of Queen Victoria. During the Victorian period, 1837 and 1901, a Gothic revival took place within the world of architecture. Homes built during this time were designed asymmetrically, with pointed arches and elaborate patterns.

Tudor homes were built between 1485 and 1603. These homes have a very distinctive, recognisable black and white appearance. Classic homes from this era often feature exposed timber frames, thatched roofs and narrow windows.

Most Brits would still like to have more modern conveniences in their homes, walk in showers, and underfloor heating, as well as making sure their homes are eco-friendly and efficient.
